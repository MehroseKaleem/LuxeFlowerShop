02

Name your photos to match

Put all product photos in one folder. Name each file so it matches what you wrote in the images column — that's the only link between the spreadsheet and the photos.

images column says:
→
red-rose-bouquet-1.jpg; red-rose-bouquet-2.jpg
folder should contain:
→
red-rose-bouquet-1.jpg
red-rose-bouquet-2.jpg
  • At least 1 photo per product, ideally 2–3 from different angles.
  • The first filename listed is the main photo shown in search results and listings.
  • JPG or PNG, and the biggest, clearest version you have — we'll optimize the size on our end.
  • No two products should reuse the same filename, even across different rows.
03

Categories to choose from

Use these exact names in the categories column. A product can belong to more than one — just separate them with a semicolon, like in the example row.

Our Collection Flowers Rose Bouquets Roses In A Box Julieta Mix Mixed Flowers Valentine's Day Collection Anniversary Special Birthday Special
● Most products should include Our Collection and Flowers alongside their specific category — that's what puts them on the general shop pages as well as the themed one.
