Privacy Policy

At Luxeflower, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when placing an order, creating an account, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting our customer service team.

  • Personal details: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Recipient information: Delivery name, address, and contact number provided for flower deliveries.
  • Payment data: Secure payment transaction details processed via encrypted payment gateways.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill your requests and improve your experience with Luxeflower:

  • Processing, fulfilling, and dispatching your flower orders.
  • Sending real-time order confirmation, tracking updates, and delivery status notifications.
  • Providing responsive customer support and resolving inquiries quickly.
  • Sending promotional offers and floral care tips (only if opted-in).

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption and restricted system access, to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with trusted service partners (such as courier delivery partners and payment processors) solely for the purpose of completing your order.

Shipping policy

All orders are shipped within 12-24 hours of you placing the order, depending on your location and the fastest available service. The typical delivery time frame is between 1-2 business days; however, you may receive your items much earlier. All orders are shipped with a tracking number, so you can track it every step of the way! Packages may be faced with delays beyond our control, such as customs or postal delays.

Delivery Locations

We provide express floral delivery across all Emirates in the UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Al Ain, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah.

Same-Day Delivery

Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 7:00 PM local UAE time. Orders placed after this time will be scheduled for delivery on the following morning.

Refund and Return Policy

At Luxeflower, we take pride in delivering fresh, handcrafted floral arrangements. Your satisfaction is our top priority, and we strive to ensure every delivery arrives in pristine condition.

1. Freshness & Quality Guarantee

Because flowers are perishable items, we handle returns and replacements with special care:

  • If your flowers arrive damaged or do not meet freshness standards, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Please provide a clear photograph of the arrangement showing the defect or damage to info@luxefloweruae.com or via WhatsApp.

2. Replacement & Refunds

Upon verification of the issue:

  • Replacement: We will send a fresh replacement bouquet free of charge on the same or next day.
  • Refunds: If a replacement cannot be fulfilled, a full or partial refund will be processed back to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Orders cancelled within 2 hours of scheduled delivery or failed deliveries due to incorrect recipient address/unreachability are non-refundable.
